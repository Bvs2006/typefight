HOW TO PLAY TYPEFIGHT

TypeFight is a 1v1 typing combat duel drawn inside a notebook.

  • Typing Speed: Faster typing delivers faster attack animations and adds up to +12 bonus damage.
  • Accuracy: Maintaining 95%+ accuracy grants a 1.25x damage multiplier!
  • Combos: Consecutive correct words increase your combo multiplier up to 1.5x damage.
  • Mistakes: Mistyped letters break your combo streak!
  • Win Condition: Deplete your opponent's 100 HP to 0 to claim victory.

Offline PvP: In Same Keyboard mode, Player 1 types with left-hand keys and Player 2 with right-hand keys.
